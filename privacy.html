Smacro

Privacy policy

Effective November 18, 2026

In short: Smacro doesn't collect your data. What you log stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Purchases go through Apple, and Smacro never sees your payment details.

What you put into Smacro

Your food log, the foods and recipes you save, your daily goal and your settings are stored on your iPhone. The developer of Smacro never receives them and has no way to see them.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, Smacro keeps a copy of your log, foods and recipes in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. This lets your log come back if you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone. That data is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and can only be read by you. The developer can't access it. You can turn this off in the Settings app under your name, then iCloud.

Barcode lookups

When you scan or type a barcode, Smacro sends just that barcode number to Smacro's barcode server, which looks it up in fatsecret's food database and sends back the food's nutrition facts. Nothing else about you or your log is sent.

The server doesn't record barcodes, and it has no accounts or personal data. Like any website, it can see the request's internet address. It keeps that in memory for about a minute, only to limit how many lookups one address can make, and never saves it. It keeps the nutrition facts it finds, not who asked for them, in memory for up to a week, so the same barcode isn't looked up again.

fatsecret only sees requests from Smacro's server, never from your iPhone. The server runs on Fly.io, which handles its connections under Fly.io's privacy policy.

Camera and photos

Smacro uses the camera to read barcodes and nutrition labels. When you choose a photo of a label, only that photo is shared with the app. Both are read on your iPhone and are never uploaded or saved by Smacro.

Backup files

If you save a backup, Smacro creates a file with your log, foods, recipes and goal and hands it to you through the share sheet. Where it goes from there, such as Files, AirDrop or email, is your choice.

Purchases

Smacro Pro and the looks, Smacritters and outfits in the Smacro Shop are sold through Apple's App Store using In-App Purchase. Apple handles the payment, your Apple Account and any refunds, under Apple's privacy policy. Smacro never receives your name, email address, payment details or Apple Account.

To know what you own, Smacro asks the App Store on your iPhone. It keeps a list of the items you own on your iPhone, so they don't show as locked while it checks again. Nothing about your purchases is sent to the developer or to Smacro's barcode server.

Apple gives the developer sales reports with totals, such as how many of each item sold and in which countries, but not who bought them.

What Smacro doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data.

Deleting your data

Deleting Smacro removes everything it stored on your iPhone. To remove the copy in iCloud as well, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, and delete Smacro's data.

Children

Smacro doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If Smacro ever starts handling data differently, for example by adding a new service, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
