Smacro

Privacy policy

Effective November 11, 2026

In short: Smacro doesn't collect your data. What you log stays on your iPhone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud account. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What you put into Smacro

Your food log, the foods and recipes you save, your daily goal and your settings are stored on your iPhone. The developer of Smacro never receives them and has no way to see them.

iCloud

If you're signed in to iCloud, Smacro keeps a copy of your log, foods and recipes in your private iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. This lets your log come back if you reinstall the app or move to a new iPhone. That data is stored by Apple under Apple's privacy policy and can only be read by you. The developer can't access it. You can turn this off in the Settings app under your name, then iCloud.

Barcode lookups

When you scan or type a barcode, Smacro sends just that barcode number to USDA FoodData Central, a free food database run by the U.S. Department of Agriculture, to find the food's nutrition facts. Nothing else about you or your log is sent. Like any website, USDA's servers can see the request's internet address. See USDA's privacy policy for how they handle requests.

Camera and photos

Smacro uses the camera to read barcodes and nutrition labels. When you choose a photo of a label, only that photo is shared with the app. Both are read on your iPhone and are never uploaded or saved by Smacro.

Backup files

If you save a backup, Smacro creates a file with your log, foods, recipes and goal and hands it to you through the share sheet. Where it goes from there, such as Files, AirDrop or email, is your choice.

What Smacro doesn't do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no selling or sharing of data.

Deleting your data

Deleting Smacro removes everything it stored on your iPhone. To remove the copy in iCloud as well, open the Settings app, tap your name, then iCloud, then Manage Account Storage, and delete Smacro's data.

Children

Smacro doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If Smacro ever starts handling data differently, for example by adding a paid feature or a new service, this page will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
