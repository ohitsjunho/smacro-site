Support

Help with Smacro, the food notebook for your macros.

Need help or found a problem?

Email contactsmacro@gmail.com. It helps to say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you tapped before the problem happened.

How do I log food?

Write what you ate on the next line of the page, like "2 eggs" or "150g chicken breast", and choose the food Smacro suggests. You can also scan a barcode, use Quick Add for calories and macros on their own, or, with Smacro Pro, take a photo of a nutrition label.

How do I keep my log safe?

If you're signed in to iCloud, Smacro keeps your log, foods and recipes there automatically, so they come back if you reinstall the app or get a new iPhone. You can also save a backup file: tap the gear at the top of the page, choose Back up your log, then Save a backup.

How do I restore a backup?

Tap the gear, choose Back up your log, then Restore from a backup and pick the file. Restoring only adds what's missing. It never changes or removes anything already in the app.

A barcode wasn't found, or Smacro says lookups are busy

Barcodes are looked up in fatsecret's food database, which doesn't list every product. When a barcode isn't found, copy the numbers from the Nutrition Facts label in by hand, or, with Smacro Pro, take a photo of the label instead. If Smacro says barcode lookups are busy, too many were made at once, so try again in a few minutes.

My totals start on the wrong day

If you eat after midnight, tap the gear and choose Change reset hour to start each day at a later hour.

What's free, and what's paid?

Writing your log, food search, barcodes, goals and iCloud backup are free, and stay free. Smacro Pro adds goal history past this week, unlimited recipes, reading labels from a photo, and Home and Lock Screen widgets. It's a yearly or monthly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. The Smacro Shop sells extra looks, Smacritters and outfits, each a one-time purchase. Your first Smacritter is free.

How do I use Apple Health?

Tap the gear, choose Apple Health and turn on the sync. Health asks what to allow. Smacro saves what you log and your weigh-ins there, and weights from a scale or another app appear in Goals. If something isn't arriving, check what's allowed in the Health app, under your profile, then Apps, then Smacro.

How do I delete my data?

Tap the gear, choose Back up your log, then Delete all data. It erases your log, foods, recipes, goal, weigh-ins and measurements, here and in iCloud. Save a backup first if you might want your log back. A backup doesn't include weigh-ins.

I bought something, but it's still locked

Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the Apple Account you bought it with. Then open Smacro Pro (tap the gear, then Get Smacro Pro) or the Smacro Shop, and tap Restore purchases.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Smacro Pro. Cancel at least a day before it renews. You keep Pro until the end of the time you've paid for. Deleting Smacro doesn't cancel a subscription.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are made through Apple, so Apple handles refunds. You can ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Is Smacro medical advice?

No. Smacro is a notebook for keeping track of what you eat, and its numbers are estimates. Its developer isn't a health professional. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before setting goals or changing how you eat. Read the terms and disclaimer.

Privacy

Smacro doesn't know who you are and doesn't keep your log on a server, and purchases go through Apple. Read the privacy policy.
