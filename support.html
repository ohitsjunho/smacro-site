Help with Smacro, the food notebook for your macros.

Need help or found a problem?

Email ohjunho725@gmail.com. It helps to say which iPhone and iOS version you have, and what you tapped before the problem happened.

How do I log food?

Write what you ate on the next line of the page, like "2 eggs" or "150g chicken breast", and choose the food Smacro suggests. You can also scan a barcode, take a photo of a nutrition label, or use Quick Add for calories and macros on their own.

How do I keep my log safe?

If you're signed in to iCloud, Smacro keeps your log, foods and recipes there automatically, so they come back if you reinstall the app or get a new iPhone. You can also save a backup file: tap the ⋯ button at the top of the page, choose Back up your log, then Save a backup.

How do I restore a backup?

Tap ⋯, choose Back up your log, then Restore from a backup and pick the file. Restoring only adds what's missing. It never changes or removes anything already in the app.

A barcode wasn't found, or Smacro says USDA is busy

Barcodes are looked up in USDA FoodData Central, which doesn't list every product and sometimes limits how many lookups it answers. Try again in a little while, or take a photo of the nutrition label instead.

My totals start on the wrong day

If you eat after midnight, tap ⋯ and choose Change reset hour to start each day at a later hour.

Is Smacro medical advice?

No. Smacro is a notebook for keeping track of what you eat, and its numbers are estimates. Its developer isn't a health professional. Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before setting goals or changing how you eat. Read the terms and disclaimer.
